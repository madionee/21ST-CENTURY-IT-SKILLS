
# Activity 6 - Making a Website

## GE 4120 - Website Creation Activity

For this activity, I created a personal portfolio website using artificial intelligence (AI) tools to explore website development, digital design, and creative self-expression.

My website showcases my personality, interests, and experiences through different sections featuring my travels, nursing life, friends, and hobbies. I designed it with a soft and elegant aesthetic, incorporating pastel colors, creative typography, personal photographs, and an editorial-inspired layout.

Through this activity, I learned how to use detailed AI prompts to develop and customize a website based on my preferred design. It also helped me improve my creativity, digital literacy, and understanding of how technology can be used to share personal experiences.

Overall, this activity encouraged me to explore new technological skills and appreciate the role of artificial intelligence in making website creation more accessible and engaging.
