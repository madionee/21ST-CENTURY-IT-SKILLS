
# Activity 5 - AI Tools

## Activity Description

This activity focused on exploring the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in creating digital content. It allowed me to experiment with AI-generated images and discover how technology can transform creative ideas into visual outputs. The activity also emphasized the importance of using AI effectively and responsibly.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how artificial intelligence can be used to generate creative digital content and enhance productivity. I also developed my skills in writing clear and detailed prompts to achieve my desired results. One challenge I encountered was getting the AI to generate an output that matched my expectations, but I addressed this by improving my instructions and experimenting with different prompts. This experience helped me understand the importance of patience, creativity, and critical thinking when working with AI tools. In the future, I can apply these skills to academic projects, presentations, and other creative activities while continuing to explore the potential of artificial intelligence.
