
# Activity 7 - Resume Generating

## Activity Description

This activity focused on using two artificial intelligence (AI) tools, ChatGPT and Claude AI, to generate a professional one-page resume based on my original resume. Both tools were instructed to summarize my qualifications, exclude references, and organize the information into a single page. I then compared their outputs based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality to determine which was more suitable for my future nursing career.

## What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how artificial intelligence can help create professional resumes by organizing and summarizing important qualifications. I also discovered that different AI tools can interpret the same instructions differently, particularly in how they prioritize clinical experience, education, and skills. One challenge I encountered was determining which generated resume presented my qualifications most effectively, but I addressed this by carefully comparing the structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality of both outputs. I preferred ChatGPT's version because it emphasized my clinical experiences and nursing responsibilities while maintaining a concise and professional layout. In the future, I can apply these skills to improve my resume by combining the strengths of different AI tools while carefully reviewing and personalizing the final document.
